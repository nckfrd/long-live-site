Privacy Policy — Long Live

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Long Live does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests of its own.

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends the honours you earn and your best results to Apple's Game Center, so they appear in your achievements and on the leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, and the game never sees or stores your Game Center identity.

What is stored on your device

Your saved game and your record of past dynasties are stored locally on your device using the platform's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not visible to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address and the date above will be revised.
