Long Live: Dynasty

How to play

Tap "Found a new dynasty" and follow the five-screen tutorial, which is also available any time from the title screen or the in-game menu (☰).

Frequently asked

My save disappeared. Saves live on the device only. Deleting or reinstalling the app removes them, and they are not carried between devices.

How do I win? Unite all of Britain, then all of Europe, then the world. Most dynasties do not, which is the point.

Does it need the internet? No. The game is entirely offline.

Is there any way to pay more? No. There are no in-app purchases and no adverts.
